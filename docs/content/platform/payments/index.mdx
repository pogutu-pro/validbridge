# Payments

ValidBridge lets an organization sell its courses using **Paystack**, with a **bring-your-own-key** model: each organization connects its *own* Paystack merchant account by pasting its API keys. Learners pay on Paystack's hosted checkout, and funds settle directly into the organization's Paystack account — ValidBridge never holds the money.

<Callout>
Payments must be enabled for your organization in [Organization Settings](/platform/organizations/settings) before the **Payments** area appears in the dashboard.
</Callout>

## How payments work

1. An admin connects the organization's Paystack account under **Payments → Configuration**.
2. You create **offers** — one-time purchases or subscriptions (monthly or yearly).
3. Each offer unlocks courses directly and/or through a **payment group** (bundle).
4. A learner clicks **Get access** or **Subscribe** and pays on Paystack's secure checkout.
5. Paystack sends a signed webhook to ValidBridge, which verifies it and grants access immediately.

## Connecting Paystack

1. In the Paystack Dashboard, open **Settings → API Keys & Webhooks** and copy your **secret key** (`sk_live_…` or `sk_test_…`) and, optionally, your **public key** (`pk_…`).
2. In ValidBridge, go to **Payments → Configuration**, click **Connect** on the Paystack card, paste the keys and click **Save & Activate**.
3. In the same Paystack settings page, set the **Webhook URL** to your ValidBridge API address followed by `/api/v1/payments/paystack/webhook`.

<Callout type="warning">
Access is granted when Paystack's `charge.success` webhook arrives. If the webhook URL is missing or points at the wrong address, learners are charged but their courses stay locked.
</Callout>

- **Test first.** Use your `sk_test_` key with Paystack test cards, then click **Update keys** and switch to your live key.
- **Security.** The secret key is encrypted before it is stored and is only used server-side. Paystack signs every webhook with HMAC-SHA512 using your secret key; ValidBridge rejects any notification it cannot verify, and ignores payments whose amount or currency does not match the offer.
- **Removing** the connection disables payments for the organization.

## Money, refunds and payouts

- **Payouts** are made by Paystack to the bank or mobile-money account configured in your Paystack account, on Paystack's schedule and fees.
- **Refunds** are issued from the Paystack Dashboard. When Paystack reports the refund, ValidBridge marks the enrollment **refunded** and removes the access it granted.
- **Payment methods** are whatever your Paystack account supports in your country — typically cards, bank payments and mobile money such as M-Pesa.
