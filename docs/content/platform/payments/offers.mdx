# Offers & Pricing

Offers are the products you sell to students. Each offer defines what students get, how much they pay, and whether the payment is recurring or one-time.

## Offer Types

- **One-time purchase** — A single payment that grants lasting access.
- **Subscription** — Recurring payments, billed **monthly** or **yearly**, that grant access while the subscription is active. Saving a subscription offer automatically creates (or updates) the matching **plan** in your Paystack account, so connect Paystack before creating one.

## Pricing Options

- **Fixed price** — Set a specific amount in your chosen currency.
- **Customer choice** — Let students pay what they want, with your amount as the minimum.

## Offer Details

Each offer includes:

- **Name & description** — What the student is buying.
- **Benefits** — A comma-separated list of what's included.
- **Amount & currency** — The price. The default currency is KES; choose a currency your Paystack account supports.
- **Public listing** — Whether the offer appears on your marketplace.
- **Access** — Courses added directly, and/or a payment group whose courses the offer unlocks.

## Public Storefront

Offers marked as public are displayed on your organization's marketplace. Each listing shows the offer details, included courses, and a **Get access** or **Subscribe** button that starts Paystack's secure checkout. Use **Preview Shop** in the Payments area to see it as learners do.
