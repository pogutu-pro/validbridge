# Branding & Landing Page

Two tabs make a ValidBridge organization feel like your own: **Branding**, which controls how it looks, and **Landing**, which controls the page people first see.

## Branding

The **Branding** tab controls the visual identity of the workspace:

- **Logo** — shown in the sidebar, on the sign-in page and in the browser tab. Use a clear logo that reads well at small sizes. SVG or PNG is ideal.
- **Colours** — the primary accent used for buttons, links and highlights.
- **Appearance** — light and dark theme treatment.

### Getting branding right

- Use one logo, and use it everywhere — consistency builds trust.
- Check your colours have enough contrast against the text they sit behind.
- Look at the sign-in page and the sidebar after changing something, since those are where branding is most visible.

Changes apply across the organization immediately.

## Landing page

The **Landing** tab controls the organization's home page — the first thing visitors and members see when they arrive.

Depending on your plan and setup you can:

- Choose which sections are shown (hero, featured courses, collections, and so on).
- Set the hero heading, subheading and image.
- Feature particular courses or collections.

### Designing a good landing page

- Say in one sentence what the organization offers and who it is for.
- Put one clear action above the fold — a course to start, or a button to browse the catalogue.
- Feature your best or newest courses, not everything.
- Keep it uncluttered; a landing page that tries to say everything says nothing.

## The navigation menu

Alongside branding and landing, the **Menu** settings tab controls what appears in the organization's navigation. Hide the areas you do not use so learners are not distracted by empty sections.

<Callout type="tip">
After any branding or landing change, open the organization home in a new tab as a learner would. Admins often see a cached or preview state that hides layout problems.
</Callout>
