# Administering

This section is for administrators and maintainers — the people who keep an organization running. It covers people and access, settings, branding, monetization, developer tools, and the audit and security controls.

- **[Users, Roles & Groups](/using/administering/users-and-roles)** — add people, control what they can do, and organise them.
- **[Organization Settings](/using/administering/organization-settings)** — general settings, navigation and AI.
- **[Branding & Landing Page](/using/administering/branding-and-landing)** — make the workspace yours.
- **[Payments & Monetization](/using/administering/payments)** — sell access to your courses.
- **[Developer & Platform Tools](/using/administering/developers)** — API, automations, custom domains, SEO and SSO.
- **[Analytics, Audit & Security](/using/administering/analytics)** — usage, audit logs, sign-in methods and two-factor.

<Callout type="info">
Administration lives in the **Dashboard**. Only roles with the right permissions can see these pages. If a section is missing, your role does not include it — the organization owner can adjust this under **Users → Roles**.
</Callout>
