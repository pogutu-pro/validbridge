# Payments & Monetization

ValidBridge can sell access to your courses through **Paystack**, using your organization's **own Paystack account** (bring your own keys). Learners pay on Paystack's secure checkout and the money settles directly into your Paystack account.

Everything lives in the dashboard under **Payments**, which has four tabs: **Overview**, **Offers**, **Payment Groups** and **Configuration**.

## The model in one paragraph

You connect Paystack, create an **offer** that unlocks one or more courses, and set a price. When someone buys, Paystack confirms the payment to ValidBridge, the buyer appears in **Overview** as a customer, and the courses unlock straight away. That is the whole loop.

## Configuration — connect Paystack

1. In the Paystack Dashboard, open **Settings → API Keys & Webhooks** and copy your **secret key** (and, optionally, your public key).
2. Open **Payments → Configuration**, click **Connect** on the Paystack card, paste the keys and click **Save & Activate**.
3. Back in Paystack, set the **Webhook URL** to your ValidBridge API address followed by `/api/v1/payments/paystack/webhook` (the path is shown on the Configuration page).

<Callout type="warning">
Do not skip the webhook. ValidBridge unlocks a course when Paystack reports the payment; without the webhook, learners are charged but stay locked out.
</Callout>

Use your `sk_test_` key and Paystack's test cards to try the full flow first, then **Update keys** with your live key. **Remove** disconnects Paystack and switches payments off.

## Offers

An **offer** is something learners can buy. To create one:

1. Open **Payments → Offers** and create a new offer.
2. Give it a name, description and optional benefits.
3. Choose **one-time** or **subscription** (monthly or yearly).
4. Choose a **fixed price** or **customer's choice** (pay what you want, with a minimum).
5. Set the amount and currency (KES by default — use a currency your Paystack account supports).
6. Under **Access**, add courses directly and/or link a payment group.

Subscription offers automatically create the matching plan in your Paystack account, so connect Paystack first.

<Callout type="tip">
Name offers by outcome, not by content — "Data Analytics Certificate Programme" sells better than "Courses 1, 2 and 3".
</Callout>

## Payment groups (bundles)

A **payment group** is a bundle of courses. Link offers to it and buyers get every course in the group — including courses you add later. Click **Sync** on a group to mirror it into a [user group](/using/administering/users-and-roles): buyers become members while their purchase is active and are removed if it is cancelled or refunded.

## Overview — your customers

**Overview** lists everyone who has bought from you: the offer, type, amount, status and date. Statuses are *pending*, *completed*, *active*, *cancelled*, *failed* and *refunded*.

## Checkout for learners

A learner clicks **Get access** (one-time) or **Subscribe** on the course or offer page and pays on Paystack. They find their purchases under **Account → Purchases** and subscriptions and payment history under **Account → Billing**, where they can cancel (access ends immediately).

If a learner says they paid but cannot get in, check **Overview**: a *pending* status almost always means the webhook is not reaching ValidBridge, or the test/live key mode does not match.

## Refunds and payouts

- **Refunds** are issued in the Paystack Dashboard; ValidBridge then marks the purchase *refunded* and removes access.
- **Payouts** go from Paystack to the bank or mobile-money account in your Paystack settings. ValidBridge does not hold or take a cut of these payments.

## Good practice

- Connect Paystack and set up the webhook before you advertise anything.
- Test the full purchase flow in Paystack test mode.
- Keep offer descriptions accurate — a mismatch leads to disputes.
- Review **Overview** after a launch to catch anyone stuck at *pending*.
