# Getting Started

This page covers what ValidBridge is, who can do what, how to find your way around, and how to get help when you need it.

## What ValidBridge is

ValidBridge is a learning platform for building and delivering courses. Everything you create lives inside an **organization** — a self-contained workspace with its own courses, people, branding and settings. You may belong to one organization or several.

The platform is organized around four ideas you will see everywhere:

- **Course** — a program of study, made up of chapters.
- **Chapter** — a module or unit inside a course, containing activities.
- **Activity** — a single piece of learning content: a rich page, a video, a document, or a gradable assignment.
- **Trail** — your personal progress record across every course you have started.

## Accounts

You sign in with an email and password, or with Google if your organization has enabled it. Enterprise organizations can also use single sign-on (SSO). Once signed in, what you can see and do is decided by your **role**.

Sessions are kept alive with JWT tokens: a short-lived access token and a longer-lived refresh token, so you stay signed in across page loads.

### Creating an account

How you join depends on the organization's signup mechanism:

- **Open signup** — anyone can create an account and join the organization.
- **Invite-only** — only people who receive an invitation can join.

Some organizations also require new accounts to be approved before they become active. If your email needs verifying, check your inbox (and spam) for the verification link.

## Roles

Every organization is seeded with four default roles. A role is really a bundle of per-resource permissions, so an admin can create custom roles and fine-tune each permission individually.

| Role | Best for | Can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User** | Learners | Browse and enrol in courses, complete activities and assignments, view their Trail and certificates, and chat with the AI Copilot. |
| **Instructor** | Teachers | Everything a User can do, plus create and edit their own courses, chapters and activities, grade assignments, and use the AI authoring tools. |
| **Maintainer** | Team leads | Manage courses, collections and published content, manage users, and moderate communities and boards. Cannot change platform-level settings. |
| **Admin** | Owners | Full control of the organization: users and roles, branding, AI, payments, developers settings, and every feature. |

<Callout>
To find your own role, open your account menu (your avatar, top-right) or go to **Account settings**. If you believe you are missing access, an admin can adjust your role under **Users** in the dashboard.
</Callout>

The dashboard itself is gated by a **dashboard access** permission. If you do not see a **Dashboard** link, your role does not include it.

For a deep dive, see [Roles & Permissions](/using/administering/users-and-roles).

## Finding your way around

There are two shells in ValidBridge: the **learning shell** that everyone uses, and the **dashboard shell** that instructors, maintainers and admins use to build and manage.

### The learning shell

This is what you see on the organization home, courses, your Trail and the rest of the learner-facing pages.

- **Left sidebar** — your primary destinations: your courses, your learning (Trail), and anything the organization has added to its menu. The logo at the top returns to the organization home.
- **Top bar** — search, the help menu, and your profile. On mobile the sidebar collapses and the top bar menu takes over.
- **Collapse handle** — the icon at the sidebar's top-right shrinks it to an icon rail when you want more room.

### The dashboard shell

Reachable from the **Dashboard** link (if your role allows it), this is the management side of the platform. Its sidebar groups everything by intent:

| Group | What lives there |
| --- | --- |
| Learning | Courses, assignments |
| Content | Library, communities, podcasts, boards, playgrounds (Labs) |
| People | Users, groups, roles, signups, two-factor, audit logs |
| Monetization | Payments — offers, groups, customers |
| Administration | Organization settings and developers tools |
| Insights | Analytics |

### The command palette

The fastest way to move around is the command palette.

1. Press **Ctrl + K** (or **⌘ + K** on macOS), or click the search box at the top of the dashboard sidebar.
2. Type a few letters — results are grouped by type (pages, courses, assignments).
3. Use the arrow keys to highlight a result and **Enter** to open it.
4. Press **Esc** to dismiss it.

### Onboarding checklist

New organizations get an onboarding checklist at the top of the dashboard sidebar. It tracks a few setup steps (create a course, invite people, brand the workspace) and disappears once you finish or dismiss it.

## Getting help

There are several ways to get help, depending on what you need.

- **Help Center (in-app)** — open **Help** in the top bar or the dashboard sidebar for a section-by-section guide to every feature.
- **Documentation** — this site, for longer-form guides and reference material.
- **AI Copilot** — ask questions about a course or activity in plain language. See [Ask AI (the Copilot)](/using/learning/ai-copilot).
- **Discord** — the community chat, for questions and discussion with other users.
- **Report feedback** — the in-app form for bugs and feature requests. Open **Help** → **Report Issue or Feedback**, describe what happened, and attach a screenshot if you can.

### How to write a good support request

1. Say what you were trying to do, and what you expected to happen.
2. Say what actually happened — include the exact wording of any error.
3. Mention where you were (page name) and your role.
4. Attach a screenshot if the problem is visual.
