# Working Through a Course

Once you are enrolled, the course **player** is where the learning actually happens. This page explains the player and every kind of activity you will meet.

## The course player

The player lays out the whole course so you always know where you are:

- **Chapter and activity list** — the left panel lists every chapter and its activities. Completed activities are ticked, the current one is highlighted, and locked ones are dimmed.
- **Main content area** — the activity you are reading or doing.
- **Next / Previous** — move linearly through the course, or jump around with the list.
- **Mark complete** — marks an activity finished and moves you on. Some activity types complete automatically when you finish them.
- **Progress indicator** — shows how far through the course you are.

<Callout type="info">
Nothing is lost if you close the tab midway. Your position and progress are saved to your account, so the player reopens where you left off.
</Callout>

## Activity types

Activities are the building blocks of a course. There are four broad kinds.

### Rich pages

The most common activity is a **rich page** written in the course editor. Pages can contain:

- Headings, paragraphs, quotes and dividers.
- Images, videos and embedded media.
- Callout boxes (information, warning, success).
- Code blocks with syntax highlighting.
- Tables, lists and checklists.
- Interactive embeds such as a video or a web page.

Read these like any article. If the author marked the page for completion, press **Mark complete** when you are done.

### Videos

A **video activity** plays a video directly in the player, with standard controls and a transcript where one has been provided. Watching to the end can mark the activity complete automatically.

### Documents

A **document activity** presents a PDF or other document inline. You can page through it without leaving the course.

### Assignments

An **assignment** is the one activity type that is graded. It asks you to produce or submit something, and your instructor reviews it. Assignments are important enough to have their own page — see [Assignments](/using/learning/assignments).

## Chapter and activity states

- **Unlocked** — available to you now.
- **Locked** — hidden or unavailable until a condition is met, such as completing the previous activity or being in a particular group.
- **Completed** — you have finished it.

## Tips for working through a course

- Use the activity list to see the shape of the whole course before you start.
- Use the AI Copilot when something is unclear — see [Ask AI](/using/learning/ai-copilot).
- Ask in the course discussion if the author enabled one.
- Revisit any activity at any time; reviewing does not reduce your progress.
