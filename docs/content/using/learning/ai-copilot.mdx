# Ask AI (the Copilot)

The **AI Copilot** is a built-in assistant that knows about your courses. You can ask it questions in plain language and get answers grounded in the actual course content, without leaving the page you are on.

It is available to learners and can be opened anywhere in the learning experience.

## Opening the Copilot

There are a few ways to reach it:

- **The Copilot button** — the sparkle/chat button that floats over course pages.
- **The top bar** — where the organization has enabled the assistant.
- **Deep link** — the help section for AI is at `#ai-copilot` if someone shares a link with you.

The Copilot opens as a drawer on the right. It can also be opened as a full page if you prefer more room.

## Asking a question

1. Open the Copilot.
2. Type your question, or pick one of the suggested prompts.
3. Press Enter and read the answer. Follow-up questions keep the context of the conversation.
4. Close the drawer when you are done; your conversation stays until you clear it.

Good questions are specific and grounded in the material:

- "Explain the difference between a chapter and an activity."
- "Summarise the key idea in the activity I am reading."
- "Give me an example of what this assignment is asking for."
- "Quiz me on the main points of this chapter."

## What the Copilot knows

The Copilot answers using the context of the course and activity you are viewing. That means its answers are about the material in front of you, not general trivia. It is a study aid — not a replacement for reading the course, and not a way to have assignments done for you.

<Callout type="warning">
Always check important answers against the course material. AI can be confidently wrong, and your instructor's own words are the final authority.
</Callout>

## When the Copilot is unavailable

If you do not see a Copilot button, AI has been turned off for your organization, or your role does not include access to it. Organizations control this from their AI settings, including which provider and model are used. Ask an administrator if you think it should be available.

## Tips

- Ask the Copilot to **explain** rather than to **answer** a graded question.
- Use it to get unstuck on terminology before asking a human.
- If an answer seems off, ask it to clarify or to point to the part of the course that supports it.
