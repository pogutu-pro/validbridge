# Boards

**Boards** are collaborative canvases — an open space where you and other members can collect notes, ideas, links and media on a shared surface. They are useful for brainstorming, planning, and gathering resources around a course or project.

## Opening a board

Boards are reached from the sidebar, from the organization home, or from wherever the board has been linked (often a course or community). Open one to see its canvas.

## Working on a board

- **Add a note** — create a note and type your thought.
- **Move things around** — drag notes to arrange them spatially.
- **Add media** — attach files, links or images.
- **Edit** — update your own notes; depending on the board's settings you may also be able to edit others'.

Changes are shared. A board is a live, collaborative surface, so everyone with access sees updates.

## When to use a board

- Collecting questions before a live session.
- Grouping references and links for a topic.
- Planning a project with your team.
- A shared "parking lot" for ideas during a course.

## Access

Which boards you can see and edit depends on your role and how the board is shared. If you can open a board but not change it, you likely have read-only access; ask a maintainer if you need to contribute.
