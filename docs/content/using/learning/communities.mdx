# Discussions & Communities

Learning is better with other people. ValidBridge gives you two ways to talk: **course discussions**, for questions about a specific course, and **communities**, for broader conversation in your organization.

## Course discussions

Many courses have a discussion area attached. It is the right place to ask a question about the material, because everyone reading the same course sees it.

- Open the course and look for the **Discussion** tab or link.
- **Start a thread** with a clear title and your question.
- **Reply** to existing threads — reading the thread first often answers your question already.
- **React** to posts to show agreement or thanks.
- Instructors and maintainers can pin useful threads and moderate.

<Callout type="tip">
Search the discussion before posting. Most questions get asked early in a course, and the answer may already be there.
</Callout>

## Communities

Communities are longer-lived spaces that may be attached to a course, a subject, or the whole organization. They behave like a forum:

- **Posts** — start a post with a title and body; add images or files where allowed.
- **Replies and nesting** — reply in threads so conversations stay readable.
- **Reactions** — lightweight agreement, useful for quick feedback.
- **Moderation** — maintainers and admins can hide or remove posts that break the rules.

## Etiquette

- Stay on topic, and keep questions in the right community.
- Be specific: say what you tried and what happened.
- Be kind. Assume the person asking is doing their best.
- Do not share answers to graded assignments. Helping someone understand is fine; handing them the answer is not.

## If you cannot post

You might be reading a community you are allowed to view but not write to, or the community may be locked. Ask an administrator about your access.
