# Courses & Enrolment

A **course** is a program of study made up of chapters, and each chapter contains activities. This page shows you how to find a course, what you see before you enrol, and how enrolment works.

## Finding a course

Courses are available in a few places:

- **Organization home** — featured and recently added courses.
- **Courses page** — the full catalogue, reached from the sidebar.
- **Collections** — curated groups of courses put together by your organization.
- **Search** — press **Ctrl/⌘ + K** and type a course name, or use the search page.

## The course page

Opening a course shows its landing page before you start:

- **Title, thumbnail and description** — what the course is about.
- **Curriculum** — the list of chapters and the activities inside them. Some activities are hidden until you reach them.
- **About the course** — author, what you will learn, and any prerequisites the author listed.
- **Resources** — any extra files the author attached.
- **Enrol button** — how you join the course (see below).

## Enrolling in a course

What happens when you click **Enrol** depends on how the course is set up:

| Course setup | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Free and open** | You are enrolled immediately and dropped into the first activity. |
| **Requires enrolment** | You are added to the course and can start. |
| **Paid** | You are sent to checkout to pay for an offer before access is granted. |
| **Invite / restricted** | You need an invitation or the correct group membership to enrol. |

<Callout type="tip">
If you are told you do not have access, the course may belong to a paid offer or to a user group you are not yet part of. Talk to an administrator about being added to the right group or offer.
</Callout>

Once enrolled, the course appears under **Your courses** on the home page and in the sidebar, and your progress is saved as you go.

## Course states

A course can be in one of these states, which affects whether learners see it:

- **Draft** — still being written; not visible to learners.
- **Published** — live and visible according to its access rules.
- **Private** — published but only reachable by people with access.
- **Archived** — retired; kept for records but no longer promoted.

## Completing a course

As you finish activities your progress increases. A course is **complete** when every required activity is done. At that point any certificate attached to the course becomes available — see [Progress & Certificates](/using/learning/progress-and-certificates).

## Leaving a course

You can unenroll from a course from its page. Your progress record is kept, so if you re-enrol later you resume where you left off rather than starting over.
