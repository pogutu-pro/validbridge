# Learning

This section is for learners. It covers everything you do as a student on ValidBridge: finding and enrolling in courses, working through activities, submitting assignments, getting help from the AI Copilot, tracking your progress, and taking part in the community.

Use the navigation to jump to a topic, or read straight through:

- **[Working Through a Course](/using/learning/activities)** — the activity view and every activity type.
- **[Assignments](/using/learning/assignments)** — submit work, understand deadlines, and see your grades.
- **[Ask AI (the Copilot)](/using/learning/ai-copilot)** — your on-demand tutor.
- **[Progress & Certificates](/using/learning/progress-and-certificates)** — your Trail and the certificates you earn.
- **[Discussions & Communities](/using/learning/communities)** — ask questions and join the conversation.
- **[Boards](/using/learning/boards)** — collaborative canvases.
- **[Podcasts](/using/learning/podcasts)** — audio series.
- **[Playgrounds](/using/learning/playgrounds)** — interactive experiences.
- **[Search](/using/learning/search)** — find anything in your organization.
