# Playgrounds

**Playgrounds** are interactive experiences in the **Labs** area of the platform. Instead of reading about a topic, you use it: a playground is a self-contained piece of interactive content your organization has published.

## Finding playgrounds

Open **Labs** from the sidebar. Playgrounds are listed as cards with a name and description. You can search the list by name or description.

## Using a playground

1. Open a playground from the Labs list.
2. Interact with it directly in the page — each playground is different, but they are all built to be explored rather than just read.
3. Your progress and any inputs are saved as you use it.

<Callout type="tip">
Playgrounds are often linked from a course activity. If a course points you to a playground, open it in the same tab and come back when you are done — your course position is remembered.
</Callout>

## Creating a playground

If your role allows it, you can create a playground from the Labs page. Give it a name and description, and it appears in the catalogue for your organization. Creating and managing playgrounds is part of the [Teaching](/using/teaching) workflow.

## Availability

Playgrounds are a feature your organization may or may not have enabled. If you do not see **Labs** in the sidebar, either the feature is off or your role does not include it — ask an administrator.
