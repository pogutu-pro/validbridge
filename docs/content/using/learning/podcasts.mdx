# Podcasts

**Podcasts** are audio series published by your organization. They are a way to keep learning while commuting, walking or doing chores — or to hear interviews, lectures and discussions in a more relaxed format.

## Browsing podcasts

Open **Podcasts** from the sidebar to see every series published to your organization. The list shows each podcast with its cover art, description, tags and episode count.

- **Search** — filter the list by name, description or tag.
- **Open a series** — click a podcast to see its episodes.

## Listening to an episode

Inside a podcast you see the episode list in order:

1. Open the episode you want.
2. Press play in the player. You can pause and resume, and the player continues if you move to another page.
3. Episodes may include show notes, links or resources below the player.

<Callout>
If your organization enabled it, you can also **subscribe** or follow a podcast so new episodes are easy to find. Look for the follow control on the podcast page.
</Callout>

## For instructors and admins

If you can create podcasts, you will see a **New podcast** button on the podcasts page. From there you can set up a series and add episodes. Creating content is covered in the [Teaching](/using/teaching) section.
