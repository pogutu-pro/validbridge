# Progress & Certificates

ValidBridge tracks everything you do. This page explains your **Trail** — the record of your learning — and the **certificates** you can earn.

## Your Trail

The **Trail** (sometimes shown as **My Learning**) is your personal progress record. Open it from the sidebar to see:

- **Courses in progress** — with a progress bar for each.
- **Completed courses** — everything you have finished.
- **Recent activity** — the activities you touched most recently, so you can jump straight back in.
- **Certificates** — any certificates you have earned.

The Trail is private to you. It is how you answer "where was I?" without hunting through the catalogue.

## How progress is calculated

Each course tracks how many activities you have completed out of the total. Some activities complete automatically (watching a video to the end, for example); others need you to press **Mark complete** or submit an assignment.

Progress is saved continuously, so you can stop at any point and pick up later. Revisiting an activity never reduces your progress.

## Certificates

Organizations can attach a **certificate** to a course. A certificate records that you completed the course, and many are verifiable online.

### Earning a certificate

A certificate becomes available when you meet the course's completion rules — usually completing every required activity, and sometimes achieving a minimum assignment grade. When you qualify, it appears on your Trail and on the course page.

### Viewing and sharing a certificate

1. Open the certificate from your Trail, the course page, or the notification you receive.
2. The certificate shows the course, your name, the date, and a unique verification reference.
3. **Download** it as a PDF, or **share** the verification link.

<Callout type="info">
Anyone with the verification link can confirm the certificate is genuine without needing an account on your organization. This is what makes it useful to employers.
</Callout>

### Why you might not see a certificate

- The course does not have a certificate attached.
- You have not yet met every completion rule.
- You are not enrolled with a role that receives certificates.

An administrator or the course's instructor can tell you exactly what is required.

## Exporting your learning record

Where enabled, you can export a record of your progress for your own files or to show a supervisor. Look for **Export** on your Trail page.
