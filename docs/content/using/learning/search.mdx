# Search

Search is the fastest way to find a course, activity, podcast, board or setting. There are two ways to search: the **command palette** for quick navigation, and the **search page** for browsing results.

## The command palette

The command palette is a keyboard-first search over the whole platform.

1. Press **Ctrl + K** (or **⌘ + K** on macOS) from anywhere, or click the search box at the top of the dashboard sidebar.
2. Start typing — results appear immediately, grouped by type.
3. Use **↑ / ↓** to move through results and **Enter** to open one, or click it.
4. Press **Esc** to close.

<Callout type="tip">
The command palette also acts as a launcher: it knows about dashboard pages, courses, assignments and settings, so "users", "analytics" or "payments" will take you straight there.
</Callout>

## The search page

For a fuller, browsable view, open **Search**. It searches across the content in your organization and lists matches with enough context for you to pick the right one.

- Type in the search box and results update as you type.
- Results are grouped by content type.
- Click a result to open it.

## What search covers

Depending on your organization and role, search can include courses, activities, assignments, podcasts, boards, playgrounds, communities and users.

## If you cannot find something

- **Check your spelling**, and try a shorter, broader term.
- **You may not have access** — search only returns items you are allowed to see. If you know a course exists but it never appears, ask an administrator about your access.
- **The item may be unpublished** — drafts are only visible to their authors and admins.
