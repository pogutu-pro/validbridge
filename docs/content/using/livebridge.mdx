# LiveBridge Live Classes

**LiveBridge** is the live classroom built into ValidBridge. A live lesson belongs to a course: lecturers schedule it from the course, students join from the same course, and attendance, questions, poll and quiz answers and the recording all stay with that course. Everything runs in the browser — nobody has to leave ValidBridge or install a meeting app.

| In the classroom | Who | Can |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lecturer** | Anyone who can edit the course | Schedule and start lessons, share screen, record, launch polls and quizzes, moderate participants, end the lesson, see attendance. |
| **Student** | Learners with access to the course | Join, chat, ask questions, vote, answer quizzes, raise a hand, react, and use mic and camera unless the lecturer turns them off. |

## Schedule a live lesson

1. Open the course and click **Schedule** in its **Live lessons** panel — or, in the dashboard, open the course's **LiveBridge** tab and click **Schedule a live lesson**.
2. Enter a **Lesson title**, the **Starts at** date and time, and an optional description.
3. Choose the recording options and click **Schedule lesson**.

- **Record automatically** — recording starts as soon as the lesson goes live (you can also start and stop it by hand).
- **Publish recordings to students** — publish the finished recording straight away; otherwise it is added as a **draft** lesson for you to review.

Students enrolled in the course see the lesson in the course and under **Live lessons** in their sidebar.

## Invite students

Click **Invite** next to a lesson to copy the lesson link or joining info, add it to Google Calendar, or email **all enrolled students** and/or up to 100 other addresses with an optional note. Anyone opening the link must sign in; people who can see the course but have not enrolled get an **Enroll & join** button.

## Start and teach

1. Click **Open** (course page) or **Open classroom** (LiveBridge tab).
2. Check your microphone and camera on the device screen (**Settings** lets you switch devices).
3. Click **Start lesson**. Students can join immediately.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Microphone / Camera | Turn your audio and video on or off. |
| Share screen | Present a screen, window or tab. While sharing, **What students see** switches between **Presentation** and **Camera**. |
| Start / Stop recording | Record the lesson (when recording is available). A **REC** badge tells everyone. |
| Panels | **Chat**, **Q&A**, **Polls**, **Quiz**, **People**, **Info** and (lecturers only) **Attendance**. |
| Leave | **Leave lesson** (it continues) or **End lesson for everyone**. |

### Engage the class

- **Q&A** — students ask questions; lecturers reply, mark them answered, dismiss or reopen them.
- **Polls** — launch a question with options and an optional time limit; students vote and then see results.
- **Live quizzes** — run any quiz task (from an assignment) or quiz block (from a lesson) in the course. Set the time per question; answers are revealed when time runs out; finish with **Show results**.
- **Raise hand** and **React** — for students.

### Manage participants

From **People**, lecturers can lower a hand, mute a microphone, stop a camera, turn off (and later allow) a student's mic & camera access, or **remove** someone — a removed student cannot rejoin that lesson.

## End the lesson and find the recording

Click **Leave → End lesson for everyone** and confirm. A recording is processed and added to the course as a **video activity** — next to the lesson's linked activity, otherwise in the last chapter or a **Live lesson recordings** chapter. Draft recordings can be reviewed with **Review draft** in the lesson report. Students find recordings under **Recorded lessons** in the course and on their **Live lessons** page.

## Attendance and reports

In the dashboard, the course's **LiveBridge** tab lists every lesson with attendees, time attended, questions, quiz average and recording status. **View report** shows one lesson in detail: attendance rate, late and early leavers, participation (messages, questions, poll votes, quiz answers, raised hands), each poll and quiz, recordings and a row per student. The **Learner engagement** view puts each student's course progress, assignments and quizzes next to their live attendance.

Attendance statuses are **Present**, **Late**, **Left early**, **Partial** and **Absent**. A student's live lessons also appear in their individual analytics under **Users**.

## For students: joining

A **Live now** banner appears while one of your lessons is live, and your sidebar's **Live lessons** item shows a Live badge. Click **Join now**, optionally turn on your microphone or camera, and click **Join lesson**. You join with both off. If the lesson has not started, the page updates automatically when it does.

## Troubleshooting

- **Microphone or camera blocked** — allow them in your browser's site settings; close other apps using the device.
- **Mic or camera button disabled** — the lecturer has turned off media for you.
- **"Reconnecting…"** — wait; the lesson resumes automatically. After **Connection lost**, click **Rejoin lesson**.
- **"You joined from somewhere else"** — click **Use this tab instead**.
- **Echo** — use headphones.
