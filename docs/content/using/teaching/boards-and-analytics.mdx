# Boards, Podcasts & Analytics

Beyond courses and assignments, ValidBridge gives you a few more tools for teaching. This page covers boards, podcasts and analytics.

## Boards

A **board** is a collaborative canvas — an open space where members collect notes, ideas and resources on a shared surface. You might use one to brainstorm with a cohort, gather references for a topic, or plan a project.

### Creating a board

1. Open **Dashboard → Content → Boards**.
2. Choose **New board**, give it a name and description, and save.
3. Open the board to lay it out using the canvas.

### Sharing a board

A board can be **public** (listed on the organization's learner-facing Boards page) or **private** (only reachable by people with access). Set this when you create or edit it. Boards are gated by the **boards** feature — if you do not see them, the feature is not enabled for your organization.

## Podcasts

A **podcast** is an audio series. Use it for interviews, recorded lectures, or a companion to a course.

### Creating a podcast

1. Open **Dashboard → Content → Podcasts**.
2. Choose **New podcast**, then set the name, description, cover art and tags.
3. Save, then open the series to add episodes.

### Adding episodes

Inside a podcast, add an episode with a title, audio file and show notes. Episodes appear in order on the learner-facing podcast page. You can add, edit and reorder episodes as the series grows.

## Analytics

Analytics tell you how learners are actually doing, so you can improve the course rather than guess.

### Course analytics

Open a course and go to its **Analytics** tab (on supported plans). Use it to see enrolment, progress and completion, and to identify where learners stall.

### Organization analytics

The dashboard's **Analytics** section gives a higher-level picture across courses: activity, engagement and trends over time.

### Assignment analytics

Each [assignment](/using/teaching/assignments) has its own analytics view for completion and scores — useful for spotting a question everyone missed.

<Callout type="tip">
Look for the activity where the most learners stop. That is usually the one worth rewriting first.
</Callout>

## How these fit together

- **Boards** — collaborative thinking and reference gathering.
- **Podcasts** — audio learning and companion content.
- **Analytics** — evidence about what is working.

Used together they turn a static course into a living program.
