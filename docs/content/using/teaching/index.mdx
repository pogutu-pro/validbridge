# Teaching

This section is for instructors, maintainers and anyone who builds learning content. It walks through creating a course, writing activities in the editor, setting up assignments and grading, using the AI authoring tools, and publishing.

Read in order, or jump to what you need:

- **[Building a Course](/using/teaching/build-a-course)** — create a course, chapters and activities.
- **[The Course Editor](/using/teaching/editor)** — write rich activity content block by block.
- **[Assignments & Grading](/using/teaching/assignments)** — build graded work and review submissions.
- **[AI Authoring Tools](/using/teaching/ai-tools)** — draft and improve content with AI.
- **[Boards, Podcasts & Analytics](/using/teaching/boards-and-analytics)** — the rest of your toolkit.
- **[Publishing & Sharing](/using/teaching/publishing)** — get your course in front of learners.

<Callout type="info">
Most building happens in the **Dashboard**. If you do not see a Dashboard link, ask an admin to grant your role the dashboard permission — see [Roles & Permissions](/using/administering/users-and-roles).
</Callout>
