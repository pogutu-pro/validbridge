# Troubleshooting & FAQ

Short answers to the problems people hit most often. If your issue is not here, use **Help → Report Issue or Feedback** in the app, or ask on Discord.

## Signing in and accounts

**I forgot my password.**
Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. Check your spam folder for the reset email. If it never arrives, your organization may manage accounts centrally — ask an administrator.

**I never received my verification email.**
Check spam, and confirm the address is spelled correctly. Administrators can resend or verify an account for you.

**I am signed in but see nothing.**
You may have joined but not yet been approved, or you may not belong to a group with course access. Ask an administrator to check your account status and access.

**I have been logged out unexpectedly.**
Sessions expire for security. Simply sign in again. If it happens constantly, clear cookies for the site and try a different browser.

## Courses and enrolment

**I cannot see a course that I know exists.**
The course may be a draft, private, or restricted to a user group or paid offer you are not part of. Ask the course's author or an administrator.

**The Enrol button sends me to a payment page.**
That course is sold as a paid offer. Complete checkout to gain access. If you have already paid and still cannot get in, check that you used the same email at checkout.

**I lost my place in a course.**
You did not — progress is saved to your account. Open the course or your **Trail** and continue where you stopped.

**A video or document will not load.**
Try refreshing, and check your connection. If it still fails, the file may have been moved or removed — report it to the course author.

## Assignments

**The submit button is disabled.**
A required task is probably still empty. Scroll through the assignment and complete every task.

**I cannot resubmit.**
Your attempts are used up, or the deadline has passed. Contact your instructor and ask them to reopen it.

**I cannot see my grade.**
Manual grading takes time. If it has been a while, message your instructor.

## AI Copilot

**I do not see a Copilot button.**
AI may be disabled for your organization, or your role may not include it. Ask an administrator.

**The Copilot gave a wrong answer.**
AI can be confidently wrong. Trust the course material and your instructor over the Copilot, and report persistent problems.

## Dashboard and permissions

**I cannot find the Dashboard.**
Your role does not include dashboard access. Ask an administrator to grant the permission you need.

**A settings tab is missing.**
Either your role lacks that permission, or the feature needs a higher plan. Ask an administrator which applies.

**I cannot edit a course.**
You may lack content permissions on that course, or you may not be a contributor. The course owner can add you under **Contributors**.

## Content and media

**An image or file will not upload.**
Check the file type and size limits for that field, and try again on a stable connection. Very large files may need to be compressed first.

**A block on a page looks broken.**
Its underlying media may have been deleted from the library. Re-upload the file, or remove and re-insert the block.

## Getting more help

When you contact support, include:

1. What you were trying to do.
2. What happened instead, including the exact error text.
3. The page you were on and your role.
4. A screenshot if the problem is visual.

That information turns a slow back-and-forth into a quick fix.
